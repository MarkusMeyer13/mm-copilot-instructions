\# Architecture Review



Act as a senior .NET solution architect.



Review the current architecture.



Focus on:



\- separation of concerns

\- domain boundaries

\- coupling

\- persistence concerns leaking into the domain

\- extensibility

\- maintainability

\- testability



Challenge the existing architecture.

Do not assume that existing design decisions are correct.



Do not modify any files.



Classify findings as:



1\. Critical

2\. Important

3\. Nice to improve



Reference relevant source files for every finding.
