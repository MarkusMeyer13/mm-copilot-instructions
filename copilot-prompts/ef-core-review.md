\# EF Core Review



Analyze the EF Core implementation.



Check:



\- entity relationships

\- indexes

\- cascade behavior

\- tracking

\- N+1 queries

\- eager loading

\- query performance

\- concurrency

\- migrations

\- database constraints



Do not modify anything.



For every finding:

1\. Show the relevant code.

2\. Explain the problem.

3\. Explain the potential impact.

4\. Propose an improvement.
